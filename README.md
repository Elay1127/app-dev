# My favorite movie: Alice in Borderland
## About the Series

**Alice in Borderland** is a Japanese science-fiction, thriller and survival series based on the manga written by Haro Aso.
The story follows Arisu and his friends as they suddenly find themselves in a mysterious version of Tokyo called the Borderland, where they must participate in dangerous games to survive.

### Why I Like It
It is exciting and unpredictable storyline, the challenging survival game got me tangled.
